## Why cash back tracking fails — and how to make sure yours doesn't

Cash back isn't automatic magic; it's a tracking chain between the portal and the retailer, and a few common habits snap it. These are the documented failure modes:

1. **You didn't start from the portal.** The click from TopCashback to Urban Outfitters is what sets the cookie. Go straight to the retailer and nothing is tracked.
2. **You used an outside coupon popup.** Even a legitimate code can break tracking if it pulls you off the retailer's site or opens a new tab. If you're using the 15% email code, type it manually at checkout rather than letting a browser extension inject it.
3. **An ad blocker interfered.** Extensions that block affiliate cookies will silently eat your cash back.
4. **You checked too early.** Cash back sits in "pending" for a few weeks to a couple of months while the retailer confirms the order (the window roughly tracks the return period). It's not "lost" — it's just not payable yet.
5. **Safari.** TopCashback itself warns that Safari doesn't reliably track cash back. Use Chrome or another browser for the actual purchase.
6. **A second account.** The new-member perks are for genuinely new members. Opening a duplicate account with a different email doesn't work.

One more small trick the portal's own guides point out: for orders under $50, Urban Outfitters charges a flat **$6.95** shipping fee, and free shipping only kicks in at **$50** (counted on merchandise only — taxes and gift cards don't count). If your cart is hovering near that line, adding the item that gets you over it usually beats eating the $6.95, and the cash back is calculated on the order either way.
